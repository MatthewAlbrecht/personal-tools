## Notes

- Smart playlist mode that adds a few tracks per matching album instead of the whole album
- Primary use: shuffle-discover unlistened For You albums (e.g. 1 or fewer listens, year filters like 2026)
- Track count per album should be configurable (one, two, etc.)
- Tracks should feel representative of the album — best songs, back-to-back bleeders, or what a fan of the album would pick
- Curation may belong in the enrichment service / AI rather than being picked at playlist-build time
